About the studio

Homes designed around the way you live.

HR Architect & Interior Design Studio is a Hyderabad-based architecture and interior-design firm specialising in elegant, functional and customised residential spaces. The studio works across apartments, independent houses and luxury villas.


Services span architectural planning, interior design consultancy, customised 3D visualisation, premium material selection, modular kitchens, wardrobes, space optimisation and complete turnkey interior execution. The approach covers conceptualisation, design development, selection, site coordination, execution and final handover.

Personalised from concept to handover.

Every project is shaped around the client's lifestyle and preferences, with attention to aesthetics, functionality, craftsmanship, quality and project timelines.
